# Billing & Subscriptions

Holostaff bills on engagement. Scanning your product, exploring the map, hiring copilots, and rehearsing them is free. You pay when your deployed copilots actually engage users.

Manage everything under **Settings → Billing & Subscription**.

## The model

- **Free to start.** Work email, no credit card. Scan, map, hire, and evaluate without a plan.
- **Pay on engagement.** Once copilots are live, usage is metered on real engagement: interventions your users actually interact with, and the sessions where a copilot talked or acted with a user. If your copilots don't engage anyone, you don't pay.
- **Your users are not seats.** You are never billed for traffic that didn't engage.

Current plans and meter rates are on the [pricing page](https://www.holostaff.ai/pricing).

## What you see in Billing

- **Current plan** — your plan, status, and billing cycle
- **Usage this period** — the engagement meters and where you stand
- **Payment methods** — cards on file, managed through the secure Stripe portal (Holostaff never stores card numbers)
- **Billing history** — past invoices with date, amount, and status, each downloadable as PDF

## Changing or cancelling

Upgrades apply immediately and prorate to your current cycle. Cancellation takes effect at the period end. A cancelled account keeps its workspace, maps, copilots, and history; only live engagement stops being served.
