# Your Account

There are two ways in. Both end in the same place: a workspace with your product on the map.

## From the terminal (recommended)

Run `npx @holostaff/cli` in your project. When the CLI needs to sign you in, it starts a device flow: your browser opens, you confirm the code, and the terminal continues. If you don't have an account yet, the same flow creates one, with a workspace ready to receive your first scan.

Credentials are stored locally in `~/.holostaff/credentials.json` (mode `0600`). Sign out any time with `npx @holostaff/cli logout`.

## From the web

Go to [holostaff.ai/register](https://www.holostaff.ai/register) and sign up with your work email and a password. No credit card is required to start.

Already have an account? Sign in at [holostaff.ai/login](https://www.holostaff.ai/login).

## Email verification

You can start scanning right away. Email verification is required only where it matters: inviting teammates and setting up billing.

## Your workspace

Every account starts with a workspace. The workspace holds your scanned products (journey maps), your autopilots, evaluation results, and impact data. Working with a team or multiple products? See [Workspaces & Collaboration](../workspaces/index.md).
