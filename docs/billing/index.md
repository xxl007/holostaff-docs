# Billing

You pay for two things. **Simulation runs** while you build and certify. **Completed handovers** when an autopilot finishes a task for a real user. Nothing else is metered.

Manage everything under **Settings → Billing & Subscription**.

## The two meters

- **Simulation runs.** Each synthetic-user run counts one unit. Runs in Holostaff's cloud count as 3. Runs from your own CI count as 1.
- **Completed handovers.** A handover bills only when the autopilot actually completed the task and the completion was verified. A handover that stops, fails, or gets handed back costs nothing.

Creation is never metered: autopilots, synthetic users, and clips are unlimited.

## Trials

Going live starts a 14-day trial. Card required; usage during the trial is free.

## Alerts and caps

You get an alert at 80% and 100% of each allowance, and at every further 50%. Handovers also carry a monthly cap: when it is reached, autopilots stop making **new** offers (a handover in flight finishes) until the period resets or you raise the cap. You are never surprised by a bill you did not see coming.

## What you see in Billing

- **Current plan** — plan, status, billing cycle
- **Usage this period** — both meters and where you stand
- **Payment methods** — managed through the secure Stripe portal; Holostaff never stores card numbers
- **Billing history** — past invoices, downloadable as PDF

## Changing or cancelling

Upgrades apply immediately and prorate. Cancellation takes effect at period end. A cancelled account keeps its workspace, maps, autopilots, and history; offers simply stop being served.
